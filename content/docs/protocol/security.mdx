## Status

Noether runs on Stellar **testnet** with test funds only. The contracts have not yet been through an external audit; one is being arranged through the Stellar ecosystem's audit programme ahead of mainnet, and the report will be linked from this page when it is published.

Every contract is open source in the [main repository](https://github.com/NoetherDEX/noether), and every deployed address on the [contracts page](/protocol/contracts) can be checked against that source on a block explorer.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security bug. Email **security@noether.exchange** (or message [@Noetherdex](https://x.com/Noetherdex)) with:

- a description and impact assessment,
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept (a testnet PoC is ideal),
- the affected contract or service, with the commit or address.

Reports are acknowledged within 72 hours and triaged within five business days. Please allow a reasonable window for remediation before public disclosure. For an exploit in progress, also contact the [SEAL 911](https://www.seal911.org) whitehat hotline.

## Scope

In scope: the on-chain contracts (`market`, `vault`, `vault_factory`, `referral`, `noether_router`, `noeracle_shim`, `noether_common`), the API gateway, the indexer, the keeper, and the web app's transaction-construction path. The Noeracle oracle is a related project; oracle write-path issues are in scope and gate mainnet.

Out of scope: findings that require a compromised device or wallet, social engineering, denial of service against the public testnet RPC, and anything already listed as a known issue in the repository's audit notes.

## Rewards

Until Noether lists on a bug-bounty platform at meaningful value locked, rewards are discretionary and scaled to severity and real impact. The full policy is the repository's [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/NoetherDEX/noether/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
