## Status

Noether runs on Stellar testnet with test funds only. The contracts have not yet been through an external audit; one is being arranged ahead of mainnet, and the report will be linked from this page when it is published.

Every contract is open source in the [main repository](https://github.com/NoetherDEX/noether), and every deployed address on the [contracts page](/protocol/contracts) can be checked against that source on a block explorer. For incident response, the contracts carry admin pause controls (the market's in two tiers, halt-open and full-freeze, so traders can always close) and in-place upgrade paths that preserve positions, orders and balances.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security bug. Email **security@noether.exchange** (or message [@Noetherdex](https://x.com/Noetherdex)) with:

- a description and impact assessment,
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept (a testnet PoC is ideal; never test against mainnet with real funds),
- the affected contract or service, with the commit or address.

Reports are acknowledged within 72 hours and triaged within five business days. Please allow a reasonable window for remediation before any public disclosure. For an exploit in progress, also contact the [SEAL 911](https://www.seal911.org) whitehat hotline.

## Scope

In scope: the on-chain contracts (`market`, `vault`, `vault_factory`, `referral`, `noether_router`, `noeracle_shim`, `noether_common`), the API gateway, the indexer, the keeper, and the web app's transaction-construction path. The Noeracle oracle is a related project; oracle write-path issues are in scope and gate mainnet.

Out of scope: findings that require a compromised user device or wallet, social engineering, spam or denial of service against the public testnet RPC, and anything already listed as a known issue in the repository's audit notes.

## Rewards

Until Noether lists on a bug-bounty platform at meaningful value locked, rewards are discretionary and scaled to severity and real impact:

| Severity | Example | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Direct theft, vault drain, oracle forgery leading to loss of funds | Up to 10% of funds at risk, capped at $10,000 to $25,000 |
| High | Forced insolvency, bad-debt injection, auth bypass | $2,000 to $5,000 |
| Medium | Griefing, denial of service against liquidations, incorrect accounting | $500 to $2,000 |
| Low | Information leak, minor misconfiguration | Swag or acknowledgement |

Good-faith research under this policy will not be pursued legally. The first reporter of a unique, reproducible issue is eligible. Fixed critical findings are published after remediation with credit (opt-out available). The full policy is the repository's [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/NoetherDEX/noether/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
